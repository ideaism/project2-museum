# Projection Audio Placeholders

Layer audio files are optional. The app starts silently, waits for visitor
interaction, and falls back to generated Web Audio textures when files are
missing or blocked.

Expected final files:

- `projection-surface.mp3`: low ceramic clink, shelf room tone, and handled archive quiet.
- `projection-middle.mp3`: radio tuning, crowd murmur, and slow route rhythm.
- `projection-core.mp3`: static, distortion, and broken redaction fragments.

Caption/transcript placeholders:

- Surface: `[caption pending] ceramic clinks, quiet room tone, soft handling sounds.`
- Middle: `[caption pending] radio tuning, distant crowd murmur, measured route pulse.`
- Core: `[caption pending] low static, clipped fragments, intermittent distortion.`

Do not add copyrighted audio unless rights are cleared. Keep final loops short,
subtle, and readable under spoken or written interpretation.
