One-page Design Statement
16 / 16
Thesis

The cup does not reveal one stable truth.

The Glitching Archive uses political ceramic mugs from V&A East Storehouse as everyday vessels of public memory. Through a mobile-first pouring interaction, visitors move between catalogue facts, speculative interpretation, redacted histories, and local contribution. The project does not claim to uncover a single hidden answer. Instead, it makes the archive's cracks, absences, and evidence boundaries visible, turning physical access into emotional and critical access.
